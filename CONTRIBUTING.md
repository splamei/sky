If you're going to contribute to this repo, please follow this guide on how to do it. If, instead, you want to contribute to the upstream Misskey repository because you feel your changes are a better fit there, please read [their contributing guide](https://github.com/misskey-dev/misskey/blob/develop/CONTRIBUTING.md)!

## DCO Sign off

When making a commit, they must be signed off and agree to our DCO (you can find it in the `DCO.md` file) otherwise your PR will be rejected. You can do this my appending `-s` when making commits. If you forget to sign off your commits, you can sign off them by rebasing your commits with `--signoff`

## PR Reviews

When making a PR, you'll need to get at least one other person with write permissions to `master` branch to approve your PR for it to merge. If your PR is rejected, we may give you the ability to get it re-reviewed provided you make the changes requested by the reviewer.

## Protected branches

Please don't push to master!! Instead please fork the repo and make a PR to the correct branch that fits your and the project's goals.
